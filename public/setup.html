Set up Munshi

Three steps. After the first one, Munshi starts recording what visitors do on your website.

  1. Paste one line into your website

    It goes just before </head> on every page.

    
    
    Copied
    
    
    
      
    WordPress
    Install the free plugin “WPCode”. Open Code Snippets, then Header & Footer. Paste the line into Header and save.
    
      
    Shopify
    Online Store, Themes, the three dots next to your theme, Edit code. Open theme.liquid and paste the line just above </head>. Save.
    
      
    Wix, Webflow, Framer, Squarespace
    Open site settings and look for “Custom code”. Add the line to the head of all pages and publish.
    
      
    Next.js or React
    Next.js: add it inside <head> in app/layout. React with Vite or CRA: paste it into index.html. Page changes inside the app are followed automatically.
    
      
    Plain HTML
    Paste it above </head> in every page, then upload.

    Tell Munshi what counts as a win
    
    
    Sign-up forms with a password field are recognised on their own. For anything else, pick whichever is easiest:
    
    
    A button counts as a win
    Add data-munshi-goal="signup" to the button or link. Use purchase for a sale.
    <button data-munshi-goal="signup">Create account</button>
    
    
    Reaching a page counts as a win
    If people land on a page like /welcome or /thank-you after they sign up or pay, tell Claude the address and it is added to munshi.config.js. Pages named thank-you, order-confirmed or payment-success already count as a sale.
    
    
    Payments, when you add them
    Your developer adds one call where the payment succeeds and one where it fails:
    munshi('purchase', { value: 499 })
munshi('payment_failed', { reason: 'card declined' })
